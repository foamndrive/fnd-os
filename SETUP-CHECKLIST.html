Changing the backend later? Redeploy, or nothing happens. Editing Code.gs and saving is not enough. The address keeps serving the old version until you press Deploy, then Manage deployments, then the pencil icon, then set Version to New version and press Deploy. Same address, new code. This trips everyone once.
The access key is the lock on your books. It goes in the app on each device you use, and nowhere else. Not in a file you upload, not in a screenshot, not in a message. If it ever leaks, run makeAccessKey() again and re-enter the new one on your devices.

Once every box is ticked, the app on your phone writes straight into your sheet, and your sheet backs itself up every night.
